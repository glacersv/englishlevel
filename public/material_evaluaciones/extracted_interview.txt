

ENGLISH PLACEMENT TESTORAL INTERVIEW
Based on the Grammar Contents of Get Involved Books 1–5
Purpose: To identify the student's approximate English level through an oral interview that progressively increases in grammatical and communicative complexity. The questions below are derived from the grammar topics provided for Get Involved Books 1–5.
Suggested interview procedure
Start at A1 and continue only while the student can understand and respond appropriately.
Do not require the student to produce the target grammar perfectly; evaluate whether the student can communicate meaning using the structure.
If a student consistently struggles with a level, stop advancing and use the previous level as the reference point.
For borderline cases, ask one or two follow-up questions from the same level before deciding.
Evaluate grammar together with comprehension, vocabulary, fluency, pronunciation, and ability to elaborate.
Quick interview question list
A1 – Book 1
What's your name? Where are you from? Can you tell me about your family?Focus: Be; possessive adjectives; possessive 's
What can you do well? What can’t you do very well?Focus: Can for ability
What do you usually do on a normal school/work day? How often do you study English?Focus: Simple present; adverbs of frequency
What do you like doing in your free time? What kind of music or movies do you like?Focus: Likes and dislikes
What are you doing these days? What do you usually do on weekends?Focus: Present progressive; simple present vs. present progressive
Describe your home or classroom. What is there? How many rooms/students are there?Focus: There is/are; countable/uncountable; how much/many
What did you do last weekend? Where were you yesterday?Focus: Past be; regular/irregular verbs; past questions
What are you going to do next weekend? What do you think will happen in the future?Focus: Will; going to; present progressive for arrangements
A2 – Book 2
What did you use to do when you were a child?Focus: Used to; simple past
What were you doing when something interesting happened recently?Focus: Past progressive; simple past; when/while
Which is more interesting: studying at home or at school? Why?Focus: Comparatives, superlatives, as...as
What do you think students spend too much time doing?Focus: Quantifiers; enough; too much/many
What do you think will happen in the next five years? What might change?Focus: Will; might
What happens if people don't sleep enough? What will you do if you have free time this weekend?Focus: Zero and first conditional
Have you ever traveled to another city or country? How long have you studied English?Focus: Present perfect; ever/never; for/since
Have you ever seen a memorable movie? When did you see it?Focus: Present perfect and simple past
What are your plans for next year? What do you think you will do in five years?Focus: Will and going to
B1 – Book 3
Tell me about an important experience from your past.Focus: Past tenses; used to; when/while
Who usually helps you when you have a problem? Who did you invite to your last celebration?Focus: Subject and object questions
How long have you been studying English? What have you already done today?Focus: Present perfect; for/since; how long; just/yet/already
What should students do to improve their English? What must people do to protect the environment?Focus: Modal verbs
What do you enjoy doing in your free time? What would you like to learn?Focus: Gerund and infinitive
Tell me about something that happened after another important event.Focus: Past perfect
Describe a person who has influenced you. What is a place that you really like?Focus: Relative pronouns; adjective clauses
How is something you use every day made? Can you describe the process?Focus: Passive voice; active and passive
What are you doing next weekend? What will you be doing this time next year?Focus: Future review; present tenses with future meaning; future progressive
What will you do if you have a difficult decision? What would you do if you could change one thing about your school? What would you have done differently in a past situation?Focus: First, second and third conditionals
What did someone recently tell you? Can you report a suggestion or request someone made to you?Focus: Reported speech; reported offers, requests, suggestions and commands
B2 – Book 4
How long have you been learning English? What have you been working on recently?Focus: Present perfect and present perfect progressive
You enjoy learning languages, don't you? What are you interested in?Focus: Question tags; questions with prepositions
Tell me about a person who has had an important influence on you. Can you add extra information about that person?Focus: Essential and non-essential adjective clauses
How do people in your family help each other? Tell me about something you learned to do by yourself.Focus: Reflexive pronouns; each other
Someone arrives late and looks exhausted. What might have happened?Focus: Modal verbs of deduction and speculation
What will you be doing in ten years? What will you have accomplished by then?Focus: Future progressive; future perfect; future modals; future time clauses
What are students allowed to do at school? What did your parents make you do when you were younger?Focus: Be allowed to; let; make
What will happen unless people change their habits? What would you do if you could live anywhere? What would you have changed about a past decision?Focus: Zero, first, second, third; unless
What do you wish you could change about your daily life?Focus: I wish; if only
Tell me about a conversation you had recently and report what the other person asked or said.Focus: Reported speech; reported questions
What activities do you avoid doing? What would you like to learn to do?Focus: Gerunds and infinitives
B2+/C1 – Book 5
Tell me about a time when one event had already happened before another.Focus: Past perfect; past perfect progressive; simple past
What will you have achieved by the time you are 30/40? What will you be doing at this time next year?Focus: Future tenses; future progressive; future perfect; future expressions
Compare two ways of learning a language and explain which differences matter.Focus: Comparing; adjective clauses
Someone didn't answer an important message. What might they have been doing? What should they have done?Focus: Modal verbs; perfect modals
Report a conversation in which someone gave you advice, asked you a question, or asked you to do something.Focus: Reported statements, questions, orders, requests and reported verbs
What would happen if your school changed its schedule? What would have happened if a past decision had been different?Focus: Conditionals; alternatives to if
What do you wish were different about society? What behavior do you wish people would change?Focus: I wish; if only; wish + would/wouldn't
What services do people usually have done professionally? What should be done to improve public spaces?Focus: Passive voice; passive with modal verbs; have/get something done
What did you use to do as a child? What are you used to doing now? What was difficult to get used to?Focus: Verb patterns; used to; be used to; get used to
